How to play

  1. One player creates a room and shares the six-character code.
  2. The artist gets a secret word and has 60 seconds to draw it—no letters or numbers!
  3. The guesser sees blank lines showing the number of letters and types guesses while the picture appears live.
  4. A correct guess gives the guesser 100 points and the artist 50 points. If time runs out, nobody scores.
  5. Players swap roles each round. After five rounds, the highest score wins.
